## Brand & Style
The design system targets high-net-worth individuals, quantitative analysts, and financial executives who require precision, high information density, and instant clarity. It bridges the deep, technical utility of modern institutional charting platforms with the polished, hyper-refined ergonomics of bespoke executive software. 

The aesthetic is precision-engineered minimalism overlaid with structural glass accents and high-contrast data visualization. The interface prioritizes content hierarchy, instant numerical recognition, and zero visual clutter. It treats financial data not merely as numbers on a page, but as live telemetry—calibrated, quiet, authoritative, and frictionless.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is built around an ultra-deep slate foundation designed to eliminate eye fatigue during prolonged analytical sessions while making directional market movements immediately legible.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Base canvas sits at `#0F172A` (deep slate). Elevated data containers and cards utilize `#1E293B` (slate-800), framed strictly with `#334155` (slate-700) hairline strokes. Higher-order floating panels or active row highlights shift into `#334155` with variable opacity.
- **Data & Text:** Critical readings and headlines use `#F8FAFC` (pure high-contrast slate). Structural labels, axes, and muted timestamps use `#94A3B8`. 
- **Telemetry & Direction:** Bullish/positive yields use sharp emerald `#22C55E`; bearish/negative trends utilize high-alert crimson `#EF4444`. Directional colors must never be used purely for decoration—their presence signifies performance.
- **Accents:** Institutional blue `#3B82F6` guides active tool selection, focused states, and key interactive pathways, while secondary indigo `#6366F1` is reserved for synthetic benchmarks, projections, and cross-asset comparisons.

## Typography
Typography is split strictly into narrative and quantitative tracks:
- **Structural Text:** `Manrope` governs all headings, body copy, navigation elements, and instructional messages. It delivers geometric neutrality with contemporary executive refinement.
- **Quantitative Telemetry:** All financial metrics, balances, rates, delta percentages, order book depths, and timestamps mandate `JetBrains Mono` with tabular numbers (`tnum`) enabled. Monospaced rendering ensures vertical optical alignment across updating values, eliminating character-shift jump during live streaming data.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-headers and system statuses leverage `label-caps` in uppercase format with wide letter spacing to maximize legibility at compact scales.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive, fluid modular grid engineered for high data density without cognitive overload.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column dynamic grid on desktop (min-width: 1280px), shifting to 8 columns on tablet (768px - 1279px), and 4 columns on mobile (< 768px). 
- **Density & Ergonomics:** Density is calibrated to compact-analytical defaults. Layout containers employ narrow gutters to allow maximum screen estate for charting canvases and multi-column telemetry tables.
- **Reflow Protocols:** On desktop, chart modules and ledger panels sit side by side in split-pane views with adjustable or fixed split ratios (e.g., 8:4 or 9:3). On mobile, multi-asset views collapse into vertically stacked modular cards with horizontally scrolling micro-metric bars.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy, distracting drop shadows, relying instead on structural surface tinting, hairline boundaries, and controlled glassmorphism.

- **Base Layer (L0):** Pure `#0F172A`, anchor for underlying window frames and fixed sidebars.
- **Card/Container Layer (L1):** `#1E293B` bounded by a strict `1px` stroke of `#334155`. Elevation is established not by physical shadow, but through crisp luminance contrast between L0 and L1.
- **Floating Overlays & Tooltips (L2):** Translucent `#1E293B` applied at 85% opacity with a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), framed with a `#334155` border and a faint, diffused ambient shadow (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Interactive Depth:** Hover states on rows or cards do not elevate along the Z-axis; instead, they trigger a border transition to `#3B82F6` or shift the internal surface background upward by 4% opacity.

## Shapes
The shape language is sharp, modern, and disciplined, utilizing soft architectural radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`) for primary cards, analytical toolbars, and inputs. This prevents the interface from feeling toy-like while preserving clean lines for dense grid alignment. 

The sole exception to the soft corner rule is the badge and filter system: status badges, ticker pills, and filter indicators utilize full pill radii (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish analytical metadata from structural card containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#3B82F6` background with `#F8FAFC` semibold text. Flat finish with subtle inset highlight; transitions to `#2563EB` on hover. Focus rings use an offset outline of `#3B82F6` at 2px.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a `1px` stroke of `#334155` and text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, the background fills with `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.4)`.
- **Destructive:** Bordered or solid `#EF4444` with subdued alpha fills for low-tier destructive actions.

### Badges & Status Pills
- Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 9999px`), displaying indicators like market change percentages or portfolio weight.
- **Bullish:** Tinted background `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`, text `#22C55E`, optional 4px solid status dot or directional delta arrow.
- **Bearish:** Tinted background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#EF4444`, directional delta arrow.
- **Neutral / Informational:** Background `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`, text `#94A3B8`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Base background `#0F172A` with a `1px` border of `#334155`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state replaces the border with `#3B82F6` and introduces an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #3B82F6`.
- Numeric input fields must enforce `JetBrains Mono` and support quick-action increment steppers or trailing currency indicators.

### Cards & Ledger Tables
- Card backgrounds use `#1E293B`, framed in `#334155`. Headers contain a unified action bar with segmented timeframe controls (e.g., 1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, ALL).
- Data tables use border-collapse models with `1px` bottom borders of `#334155`. Row hover states apply a subtle fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`. Numerical figures right-align strictly to ensure decimal tracking across rows.

### Micro-charts & Sparklines
- Chart areas embedded directly into cards and table rows. Sparklines have a `1.5px` stroke with no terminal dots except at the final live data point.
- Gradient fills below sparklines use a vertical fade from `20%` color opacity at the peak to `0%` opacity at the baseline.
- Positive trends render in `#22C55E`; negative trends render in `#EF4444`. Crosshairs on scrub display a vertical hairline dashed rule (`#94A3B8`) with a glassmorphic floating telemetry badge.
